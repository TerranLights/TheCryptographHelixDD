# The DNA Mafias — Overview

## Origins

In the beginning, unholy unions of corporation and government monitored, logged, and analyzed every aspect of what people did. A ragtag team of hackers arose — people who encrypted, hashed, and obfuscated others' personal information (search history, purchase history, etc.) to protect common people from being spied on. They started charging for their services, carved out a profitable niche, and with time their power grew.

They began trafficking data. Eventually they built entire shellworld mini-planets around Jupiter to run illicit activities. First they trafficked via plants, then animals, then humans — breaking sensitive code into blocks spread across multiple organisms to hide their tracks.

Some within the Mafia began questioning the ethics of what they were doing.

## General Character

The Mafias are not simply evil. They arose to protect people from oppressive surveillance. Their corruption is a tragedy — a corruption of a genuinely protective impulse.

**Mafiosos are honest** because there are life-and-death consequences for crossing people. Politicians face no such consequences. This creates a dark comparison: the criminals have more integrity than the law.

The Mafias follow the 48 Laws of Power by necessity — it's a survival framework in a world of deadly consequence.

- **Phase 3 (Power)** — Ceasefire breaks; open warfare; a mafioso refuses to kill his mother; outer worlds declare independence
