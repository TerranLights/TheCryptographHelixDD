# Outer Tepenia

The outer solar system territories — collectively called "Outer Tepenia" in current usage. This name replaced the earlier working name "Terragenesis" (which is being saved for future repurposing; see `reference/future-ideas.md`).

At the series' opening, Outer Tepenia is not yet colonized. Jupiter colonization begins during the events of the story.

---

## Mars Events (Outer Tepenia Seed Material)

*The following events are associated with the Martian colonies — seeds of what will become Outer Tepenia culture. These are early-game world-building events, probably useful for Books 1–3.*

- **GeoCistern upgrade offer** (New Szolnok): A wealthy donor offers to upgrade the city's GeoCistern for free, in exchange for a "slight" change in economic policy toward a more industrial model. [Decision: accept]

- **Viral programmer** (Neo-Debrecen): A talented programmer is rapidly gaining popularity across Mars. Promotion options: paid broadcasts (+1,500c in Neo-Debrecen) or live shows (+5/min population in Neo-Debrecen).

- **Democratic immigration policy**: Citizens of Mars love their new home, but people elsewhere still suffer. Adopting a more democratic government could encourage solar-system-wide immigration, boosting population growth in every city.

- **The Face in the Mountains** (New Turku): During an intense dust storm, over a dozen travelers reported seeing a huge stone face carved into mountains near New Turku. After the storm it couldn't be relocated. Witnesses insist something is out there.

- **The Hiker** (Neo-Zalaegerszeg): A humanoid figure was seen walking freely on the surface without protection — a local legend has developed about "the Hiker," a reclusive human (or alien?) surviving alone on the Martian surface.

- **Hit TV show** (New Szolnok): A TV show portraying New Szolnok as glamorous and exciting caused a minor population boom. People flocked to Mars hoping to live that fantasy.

- **Iridium deposits** (New Szeged): Rich iridium deposits discovered throughout the New Szeged region. Prospectors are flocking to the site.

- **Hab Dome degradation** (Neo-Marosvásárhely): During a routine inspection, engineers discovered fungus colonies that downgraded the hab dome from Level V to Level IV.

- **Organized crime** (New Szolnok): A rash of organized crime spread through New Szolnok. Local police are struggling to contain it. (First signs of the Mafia's presence on Mars.)

- **New Rovaniemi architecture**: A new architectural style is developing — stone surfaces and geometric proportions. Quickly becoming associated with New Rovaniemi in the public eye.

- **Sons of Hephaestus** (Neo-Debrecen): A protestor is spreading an anti-terraforming message and swaying impressionable young people. [Decision: hire an assassin, or let them protest?]
