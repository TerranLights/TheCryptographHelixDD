Argument against transformation:	// the characters intrinsic reason for refusing the call
	

Doorway of no return #1:	// the point after which the character absolutely cannot go back

Mirror Moment [Midpoint]:	// metaphorically looking at himself in the mirror; most commenly in the middle of a character's arc
	:: happens around either the end of "Find" or sometime during "Take" :: essentially "Apostasis"
	> self-confrontation
		> Who am I?
		> What have I become?
		> What MUST I become?
	

Pet the dog:	// very close to the Mirror Moment; could be on either side of it; takes a moment out of the serious danger he's in to help another character weaker than himself
	

Doorway of no return #2:	// something happens which makes the resolution inevitable
	:: usually one of four things:
		> A major crisis
		> A major setback
		> A major discovery
		> A major clue

--- ACT 3 ---

Mounting Forces:	// outside forces are working ever increasingly against the protagonist
	

Lights Out:		// this is when things seem darkest for the lead; all possible choices are bad ones, yet the protagonist must choose
	

Q Factor:		// an emotional jolt that comes at the right time to give the protagonist the courage needed for the final battle; this is set up in Act 1; either before or after "Lights Out"; often tends to be connected to the "Care Package"
	:: could be:
		> a physical object that reminds the character of something important
		> recalling the words of a trusted friend or mentor
		> rebuke from someone with moral authority
