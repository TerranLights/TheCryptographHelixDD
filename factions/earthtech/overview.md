# Earthtech — Overview

> **"Earthtech" is a placeholder name.** See `reference/future-ideas.md` for 14 renaming candidates. Other early internal name: **Equitech**.

## What It Is

The Silicon Valley tech-giant oligarchy that has become Earth's planetary government. Countries technically exist. Elections technically happen. Both are formalities. The tech giants are the real power — they run civilization like a company. At the top there is just a board meeting.

Headquartered in San Francisco. Philip K. Dick-inspired in tone and structure: mundane evil, bureaucratic totalitarianism.

## Origins

Initially a cluster of different companies with a huge internal power struggle to determine who would become Chancellor. That power struggle is part of the story's backstory and Howie's arc.

## What They Do

- Mind-upload narcissists into android bodies as a form of "immortality" (copy-paste, not cut-paste — the original dies, the copy lives on)
- Run a planetary surveillance state via the **psychological cell algorithm** and the **piano-key computer** brain-wave decryption system
- Monitor citizens through chips and IoT/local spying
- Operate **floating gulags** above the atmosphere of Venus — political prisoners mine there
- Use Earth Law-Enforcement as their military/police arm

## Character

Everyone in Earthtech has a negative "Mirror Moment" — they each decide to go even further into evil because they believe that living authentically will kill them. They are the carnal embodiment of narcissism.

Their leadership consists of high-level androids with mind-uploads of people who once considered themselves gods. The technology is crude and rocky in the present day (from the reader's perspective), but 200+ years from now it is possible enough to be horrifying.

This calls into question: are humans already like robots? If there is no physical soul, then consciousness is only brain circuitry — and mind-upload is "copy-paste," not "cut-paste." The original dies. The copy lives on. Those are two separate consciousnesses.

Earthtech refuses to acknowledge any of this philosophical complexity. They are too egotistical (in Jungian terms: Satanic — incapable of acknowledging their own Shadow) to admit it.

## Antagonist Role

Earthtech is the series' primary antagonist. Their trajectory across the 7 books:
- Books 1–2: Surveillance state; mining Venus; spying on Mars; developing the piano-key computer
- Books 3–5: Escalating enforcement; funding anti-Mafia campaigns; using Mafia habitats as prison worlds
- Book 6: Full-scale assault on all Mafias
- Book 7: Bomb drops on Mars. Final battle. Earthtech annihilated.
