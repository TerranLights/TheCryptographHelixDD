# Book 1 — *Terranocracy Anointed*

- **Theme:** Privacy
- **KWML:** Passive Shadow — King (Good Times)
- **Societal Cycle:** Good Times (prince moves into precocious child)
- **Primary Locations:** Mars, Earth (also: Venus)
- **Monomyth Step:** You (Iteration 1)
- **Antagonist Against Freedom:** The God Complex
- **Harmon Step (series level):** You

## Topic

Earthtech having control over the people of Mars.

## Notes

- The Fibonacci Flower and its encrypted message ("They're killing us.") is the series' Care Package / MacGuffin — returns in Book 7 as the Q-Factor.
- This book establishes Venus as the place of suffering that haunts the entire series. The series' opening image is set on Venus; the closing image of Book 7 is set on Earth.
- First book in the **Privacy** theme arc (Books 1–2).
- Volume 1 ends at the Midpoint. Volume 2 runs from post-Midpoint through the Finale.
