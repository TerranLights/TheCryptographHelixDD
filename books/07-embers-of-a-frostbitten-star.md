# Book 7 — *Embers of a Frostbitten Star*

## Overview

- **Theme:** Power
- **KWML:** Light/Completion — Warrior (Strong Men → Good Times, late, on Earth); Warrior
- **Societal Cycle:** Strong Men → Good Times
- **Primary Locations:** Mars, Earth (also: Venus, the Belt, the starlifters)
- **Monomyth Step:** Return/Change (Iteration 7 + Completion)
- **Antagonist Against Freedom:** Human Extinction
- **Harmon Step:** Return → Change

## Overall Topic

Earthtech, the various Mafias, and the Resistance against each other in a total power struggle. Ends with Hungarians leading the return to Earth.

**Crossing the Return Threshold / Doorway to Act 3:**
- **The first Earthtech bomb drops on Mars**

**Mounting Forces:**
- *TBD*

**Lights Out:**
- Freedom being total self-actualization (at the individual, planetary, and every scale in between), "Lights Out" would be a near-total lack of self-actualization — near-total willing, voluntary submission.

**Q Factor:**
- A physical object that reminds the character of something important:
  - **The Venus mines (gulags)** — the place it all started
- A rebuke from someone with moral authority:
  - What has moral authority over freedom? **Responsibility**
- Someone who is stranded somewhere is accidentally found by somebody else who has the **Fibonacci Flower** from the Care Package in Book 1

**Final Battle:**
- Humans integrate the memes (their collective Shadow)
- Convince the memes to integrate their own Shadow
- Together they "unite" to fight Earthtech — who are still too egotistical (Satanic, in the Jungian sense) to even admit they have a Shadow

**Transformation:**
- Humans and memes find a symbiotic harmony with each other

**Master of Two Worlds:**
- Humans understand that they are part of something greater than themselves
- The memes understand that they cannot exist without humans
- Both have become fully self-actualized

**Freedom to Live:**
- Earthtech having been fully annihilated, the humans of Mars are now free to return to Earth
- **Hungarians lead this process of terrestrial resettlement**

## Notes

- Second and final book in the **Power** theme arc (Books 6–7).
- The Fibonacci Flower returns here as the Q Factor — it was set up in Book 1 as the Care Package.
- This is the completion of the entire monomyth arc: "Freedom to Live."
- The opening sentence of the series is set on Venus (Book 1). The ending is set on Earth (Book 7). The journey is from the prison of Venus to the freedom of Earth.
