## Topic

A total power struggle across all factions; the dispersal of humanity across the solar system.

## Known Ending

The series ends with three population streams:
- Some humans and robots return to Earth
- Some remain on Mars
- A moderately substantial number colonize Jupiter (seeding what becomes the Outer Tepenia 1 civilization ~1,000 years later)
- Some are in transit toward Saturn

This dispersal IS the defeat of the Human Extinction antagonist — humanity cannot be exterminated when it occupies four distinct points in the solar system.
