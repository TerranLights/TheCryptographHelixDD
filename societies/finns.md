# Finns

## Overview

Finland survived the demographic and geopolitical upheaval that erased or transformed many neighboring countries. Being far north kept them insulated. While Sweden became Suwedistan and Germany became al-Manya, Finland retained its identity.

## Role in the Story

A metal band composed of either Finns or Hungarians plays a role during a crisis (to be determined). Finns are connected to Mars colonization — New Rovaniemi and New Turku are among the Martian cities, suggesting Finnish cultural presence in the colonies.

## Cultural Traits

Sisu (Finnish: extreme perseverance and grit). Insularity, self-sufficiency, and a deep relationship with nature and technology — qualities that translate well to survival in extreme environments like Mars.

## Notes

- Their presence in Mars city names (New Rovaniemi, New Turku) suggests they were part of the original colonial wave alongside Hungarians
