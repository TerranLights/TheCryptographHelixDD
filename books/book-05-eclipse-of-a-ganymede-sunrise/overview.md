# Book 5 — *Eclipse of a Ganymede Sunrise*

- **Theme:** Ethics
- **KWML:** Active Shadow — Lover → Warrior (Hard Times → Strong Men)
- **Societal Cycle:** Hard Times → Strong Men
- **Primary Locations:** Jupiter (also: Saturn)
- **Monomyth Step:** Find (Iteration 5)
- **Antagonist Against Freedom:** The Chaos of Anarchy
- **Harmon Step (series level):** Find

## Topic

Isolationism and the ethics of lawlessness in the Jovian metasystem.

## Notes

- Third and final book in the **Ethics** theme arc (Books 3–5).
- All Jovian shellworlds are fully functional by this book.
- Mining bases on Titan and other Saturnian moons are now established.
- The ceasefire that ends this book is extremely fragile — it breaks catastrophically in Book 6.
- Volume 1 ends at the Midpoint. Volume 2 runs from post-Midpoint through the Finale.
