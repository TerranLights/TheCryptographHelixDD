# E-thots — Overview

## What They Are

E-thots (also called "f-girls" in Russian Mafia parlance) are a class of performance/sex-work professionals operating across the solar system. They are not a unified faction but a loosely connected social category. Some are independent; some are trafficked or under contract with the Russian Mafia.

The most developed individual in this category is the Colombian e-thot — see `factions/e-thots/colombian/overview.md`.

## Nationalities

The e-thot network spans many nationalities. Those identified so far:
- Colombian (most developed)
- Russian
- Ukrainian
- Japanese
- Czech
- Chinese
- Brazilian
- Mexican

## Seduction Archetypes (from Robert Greene's *Art of Seduction*)

Different e-thots embody different seduction archetypes. The Colombian embodies primarily **The Star**.

### The Siren
- Offers a total release from the limitations of life
- Her presence is always heightened and sexually charged
- Transports the man to a realm of pure pleasure
- Takes control of the male libido by embodying his fantasy

### The Ideal Lover
- Thrives on people's broken dreams, which become lifelong fantasies
- Is an artist in creating the illusion you require
- Wields limitless seductive power in a world of disappointment
- Offers romance, adventure, and lofty spiritual communion

### The Dandy
- Is more fluid than we are
- Creates her own persona
- Excites us because she cannot be categorized
- Hints at a freedom we want for ourselves

### The Natural
- Represents the golden paradise of childhood
- Embodies spontaneity, sincerity, unpretentiousness
- Gets us caught up in her playful spirit
- Neutralizes people's defensiveness and infects them with helpless delight

### The Coquette
- Delays satisfaction, holding the victim in thrall
- Orchestrates a back-and-forth movement between hope and frustration
- Baits with the promise of reward

### The Charmer
- Seduces without sex
- Is a consummate manipulator
- Masks her cleverness by creating a mood of pleasure and comfort
- Deflects attention from herself and focuses it on her target
- She understands your spirit, feels your pain, and adapts to your moods

### The Charismatic
- Has an inner quality of self-confidence, sexual energy, sense of purpose
- Heightens her charisma with a piercing gaze, fiery oratory, an air of mystery
- Radiates intensity while remaining detached

### The Star
- Offers an escape from the harshness of life in fantasies and dreams
- Stands out through a distinctive and appealing style
- Makes us want to watch her
- Vague and ethereal, keeping her distance
- Lets us imagine more than is there
- Has a dreamlike quality that works on our unconscious
- Projects a glittering but elusive presence

## Narrative Role

E-thots exist at the intersection of the Russian Mafia's trafficking network and the personal lives of characters across all factions. The Colombian e-thot in particular becomes a pivot character who bridges the Mafia world and the Resistance.

The concept of "selling copies of yourself to high-paying customers" is literally enabled by DNA technology in this era.
