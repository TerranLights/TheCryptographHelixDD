# Germans

## Overview

Germany is one of the significant cultural groups in the Tepenian off-world civilization — the Gini-adjusted census places Germans as the leading European contributor to the Tepenian exile wave. Their descendants are a meaningful presence in the solar system civilization of the ~3000s.

The "al-Manya" name was from the original standalone-universe concept and does not apply in the Tepenian universe. What Germany and its descendants look like by the ~3000s — both on Earth under Earthtech/The Mandate, and among the off-world Tepenian descendants — is to be developed.
