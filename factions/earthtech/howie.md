# Howie

## Who He Is

Howie is **the** technovillain. Not simply "a Silicon Valley government official named Howie," but a man who forcibly makes himself an "admin" (or whatever the future term is) to #Cancel anyone he finds disagreeable.

He is the **Grand Chancellor of Silicon Valley** — the top of the Earthtech hierarchy.

## Origin

He starts at the bottom, back on Earth, as a lowly "community standards" content moderator. He works his way up — forcibly, violently — to Grand Chancellor.

> *"Either you face my mercenaries or you make me president! You idiots should all be beheaded. I can't stand the stupidity."*

## Methods

- **Bribery** — bribes people to advance up the hierarchy
- **Intimidation / violence** — when bribery fails
- **Cancellation** — uses his admin power to silence and destroy anyone who disagrees

He is "the perfect technovillain" — not the mustache-twirling variety, but the mundane, petty, corporate kind that makes Silicon Valley power feel like bureaucratic evil rather than dramatic villainy.

## The Mirror Moment He Refuses

Like all Earthtech leadership, Howie has a negative Mirror Moment — he looks at himself and chooses to go further into evil rather than face authentic self-confrontation. He believes living authentically would kill him.

## Backstory Connection to the Story

- One of the Venus inmates who helps the story's protagonists is a former Earthtech employee who was betrayed by Howie
- That inmate sends an encrypted password from Venus to Mars (Book 1 — Supernatural Aid beat)

## Notes

- The Earthtech power structure initially had multiple companies in a power struggle before Howie consolidated control
- He represents the "God Complex" antagonist of Book 1
