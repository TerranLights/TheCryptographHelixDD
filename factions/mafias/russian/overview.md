# Russian Mafia — Overview

## Identity

**The first DNA Mafia to form.** The Russians are the original hackers who began the whole movement.

**Techno-Primitivists by design** — intentionally low-tech as a security strategy. They protect themselves from hacking by using technology that hackers don't bother with: waveforms as analogues to SHA256SUM. Light-based computer interfaces similar to today's computers (rather than advanced DNA-based systems).

## What They Traffic

- **E-thots ("f-girls")** — they control a network of sex-work performers across the solar system
- **Framejacking drugs** — custom-synthesized hallucinogenic framejacking schematics, DNA-encoded for distribution
- **Meme-warfare** — the Russian Mafia are the **very first to weaponize memes** as an offensive tool

  - A member of the DAMRAGE cult (akin to Buddhist personal growth; he refused mafia life because of it)
  - The boyfriend/lover of the Colombian e-thot's sister
  - This killing becomes a major catalyst for inter-Mafia war

- **Colombian e-thot recruitment** — The Russian Mafia approaches the Colombian to work exclusively for them

## Technology Specialty

Low-tech by choice. Waveform hashing. Light-based interfaces. Their low-tech approach is what makes them resilient and hard to hack. The Chinese, by contrast, use cutting-edge brain-encryption — and the Russians exploit it.
