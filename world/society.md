# Society

## Political Structure

Countries and governments technically exist but are a formality. The tech giants — currently called "Earthtech" (placeholder; see `reference/future-ideas.md`) — are the real planetary government. They run civilization like a company: at the top there's just a board meeting.

## Nations and Ethnic Groups (Earth-Era)

By the Cryptograph Helix era (~3000s), centuries of demographic and political change have reshaped the map of Earth. The specific forms these nations take needs to be developed from the Tepenian universe's established demographics — the educated, wealthy, robot-owning classes of these nations largely went into Antarctic exile in 2564, and what remained evolved over the following 500 years under Upper Earth and then Earthtech/The Mandate control.

**Confirmed:**
- **Finland** — survived and maintained cultural cohesion; geographic isolation was a factor
- **Iceland** — survived; stopped doling out welfare long ago, which insulated it from demographic pressures
- **Hungarians** — backbone of the Resistance / Altay Nation; see `factions/resistance/overview.md`
- **China** — collapsed before the story began due to catastrophic birthrate decline; its successor is the Sinian Federation, which sent one of the largest exile waves to Tepenia

**To be developed:**
- What do Germany, the UK, Sweden, and other European nations look like in the ~3000s under the Tepenian universe's demographic logic? The Suwedistan / al-Manya / The Caliphate names were from the original standalone-universe concept and do not apply here. These nations' fates need to be rethought from the ground up.

## Music and Subculture

- A "disciplinary fellowship" within Metal music formed after Lemmy (Motörhead frontman)
- By the ~2500s, **"Lemmy"** has become the common English word for "cheers" (a toast)
- A metal band — composed of either Finns or Hungarians — helps people during a crisis of some kind (to be determined)

## Medical System

Blood "transfusions" work via network: upload your blood to a computer network for analysis. After analysis, receive a patient file number. Use it to book doctor's appointments. Medical history is stored on the network.

## Other World Details

- **Domesticated bats** exist as pets or utility animals
- **"Trouvatki"** — a ship name one character may use (derived from a Russian/Slavic root)
- **Liberal hiveminds in a Matrioshka brain** are referenced as an extreme edge case of digital culture
- **Anicetus** — a semi-constructed city/ship, built partly from Belt and Jovian resources
- The idea of "STEM achievements seeming like magic to primitive people" is a theme in how Earthtech presents itself to colonized populations
- J. Campbell's "Tomb-of-the-Womb to the Womb-of-the-Tomb" — explore what it's like for a baby inside the womb; follow the KWML template; a possible structural element

## The DAMRAGE Cult

A philosophy or religion (akin to Buddhist personal growth and conservative values) present on the starlifters. Referenced in Book 6. The innocent Russian man killed in the Chinese IoT assassination was a member of DAMRAGE and a childhood friend of a Russian Mafia boss.
