# Hungarians

## Overview

Hungarians are the backbone of the Resistance — formally called "the Altay Nation" by outsiders, though they never actually call themselves that or think of themselves as properly unified. They also include Mongolians and Kazakhs as part of their coalition (reflecting historical Altaic linguistic and cultural connections).

They settled Mars and named its cities in Hungarian. Their cultural identity persisted strongly across the centuries of diaspora.

## Martian Cities

- New Rovaniemi

- Neo-Zalaegerszeg

## Role in the Story

The Hungarians are the primary protagonists of the series, embodying Freedom as a lived experience. By Book 7 they lead the return to Earth after Earthtech's defeat.

Key character: **László** — see `characters/laszlo.md`

## Cultural Traits

A sense of fierce independence and cultural stubbornness that survived millennia of domination by outside powers. This quality translates naturally to anti-surveillance, anti-totalitarian resistance in the Cryptograph Helix era.

## Coalition: The Altay Nation

The coalition also includes **Mongolians** and **Kazakhs** — peoples with shared historical Altaic roots. Outsiders collectively call this alliance "the Altay Nation," though the groups themselves never formally adopt that name.
