## "Terragenesis"

**The name "Terragenesis" sounds cool and should be repurposed for something in the story.**

It was originally used as an informal placeholder name for what is now called **Outer Tepenia** (the outer solar system territories). That meaning has been retired — Outer Tepenia is the official term now.

Possible new uses for "Terragenesis":
- A corporation or megaproject involved in Mars or outer-world terraforming
- A ship name
- A political movement or independence faction
- A scientific program (perhaps the original Earthtech terraforming initiative)
- A cult or splinter religion

## Earthtech Naming Candidates

The name "Earthtech" is a placeholder from early development. Below are candidates to replace it. The entity is a "global Silicon Valley-igarchy" — countries and governments technically exist but are a formality. The tech giants are the real power. Think: oligarchic board running the world like a company. Philip K. Dick-inspired. Headquartered on Earth (San Francisco).

Also considered: **Equitech** (the earliest internal name, found in old notes).

### Mundane / Corporate-Sinister

**The Board** — the world is run like a company; at the top there's just a board meeting. Dark humor; insultingly mundane for something totalitarian.

**The Directorate** — old word for a governing board; executives, not politicians. Implies corporate management structure over civilization.

**The Consensus** — sounds benign and democratic, is neither. Very Silicon Valley ("we reached consensus").

**The Forum** — ancient Roman term for civic participation, repurposed as oligarchic control. Ironic distance between name and reality.

### Ironic / Subverted

**The Commons** — deeply ironic; the "commons" owned by the least common people imaginable.

**The Accord** — sounds like a peace treaty or diplomatic agreement. Actually a control structure.

**The Mandate** — authority built right into the name; zero ambiguity about who's in charge.

### Silicon Valley Cultural

**The Valley** — used as a proper noun, the way people already use it. Familiar, almost casual; the scariest kind of power.

**The Network** — they literally are one; also surveillance-coded.

**The Stack** — tech infrastructure term; "the full stack" of civilization. Insider jargon that has absorbed everything.

### Classical / Latin

**Civitas** — Latin for "city-state / citizenship." Implies the city-state ate the world; civilization as product.

**The Platform** — extremely Silicon Valley; how tech companies already talk about themselves. Terrifyingly mundane; very PKD.

### Cold / Impersonal

**The Apparatus** — bureaucratic, impersonal; PKD-appropriate. Implies machinery larger than any individual.

**The Sovereign** — singular, cold, post-national. No plural, no council — just The Sovereign.
