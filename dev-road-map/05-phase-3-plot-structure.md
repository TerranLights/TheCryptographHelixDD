## Beat Sheet Standard

A book beat sheet is Phase 3 complete when it contains:
- Inciting Incident
- Act 1 Turning Point
- Midpoint / Mirror Moment
- All Is Lost
- Act 2 Turning Point
- Climax
- Resolution / setup for next book
- Per-faction position at the end of the book (where are the Mafias, Memes, Earthtech, Resistance, and László at book's end?)

## Parallel Plot Interlock Check

Once Books 2–6 beat sheets exist, run through each parallel-plot Harmon arc and confirm:
- The step timings make sense relative to book divisions
- No two parallel plots reach their "Pay the Price" step in the same book (dramatic collision, not dramatic redundancy)
- The Resistance arc and László's arc don't peak at the same moment unless that's intentional
