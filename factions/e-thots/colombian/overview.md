## Who She Is

Renowned and loved throughout the solar system for her:
- High-level intelligence
- Impeccable conversational skills
- Other-worldly beauty

She is similar in function to a Japanese courtesan — able to converse on a wide variety of topics at an elite level. She is not merely a sex worker; she is a high-status intellectual companion.

She is ultimately **one of the central figures in the Resistance**, using her charms and her neuroframe drugs to get information across enemy lines.

**Seduction archetype: The Star** — offers escape from the harshness of life in fantasies and dreams; elusive, dreamlike, vague but magnetic.

## Arc Summary

**Act 1 — Setup:**
- Approached by a Chinese mafioso to do an e-thottery sex trip as a "present" for his painfully shy younger brother (who just turned 16)
- She includes a loving ASMR-style bonus because she feels sorry for the kid
- Approached by the Russian Mafia to work exclusively for them
- She refuses — she can do quite well freelancing; she's renowned across the solar system and doesn't need them
- Earthtech wave-records parts of her brain for data-mining. She's freaked out. This makes her want to help the Mafia to keep the contents of her thoughts safe from Earthtech spyware.
- **Doorway of No Return #1:** Earthtech's brain-scanning drives her toward the Mafia

**Act 2 — Confrontation:**
- Gets the Russian Mafia to encrypt her brain using the SCN/TMN "private key" (STN). The initial neural encryption distorts her perceptions of reality.
- **Mirror Moment:** She is absolutely appalled at the IoT assassination of an innocent Russian man. The Chinese mafioso who ordered the killing is also one of her best clients. She confronts herself: *Who am I? What have I become? What must I become?*
- **Doorway of No Return #2:** Russian meme-warfare ends up causing a psychoschizoid break in her brother. She decides to switch sides and help László and the Hungarian Resistance.

**Act 3 — Resolution:**
- She acts as a double agent, using her networks and her position in the Mafia world to relay intelligence to the Resistance
- She gets a Hungarian memelord to help her relay messages to László

## Her Sister

Her sister's lover is the innocent Russian man who was killed in the Chinese IoT assassination. This personal connection makes the political crisis viscerally personal for her.
