## Framejacking (Brain Augmentation)

*(Note: "Framejacking" is a term coined by Dennis E. Taylor — the in-universe term may need to differ. Consider renaming.)*

DNA-encoded schematics for time-dilation brain augmentation. Reference: `world/technology.md`.

**Valuable for:**
- Fighting a bunch of guys who are all attacking you all at once
- Criminals who want to kill a bunch of people all at once
- Spending months to think about something that needs to be decided within minutes
- Feeling an orgasm that lasts for months
- Gamers who want to win tournaments

**Dangers:**
- Burns your brain out — must be used very, very sparingly, or it turns your brain to jelly
- Due to its highly neurotropic properties, it can be very addictive, causing a societal scourge of addiction
- Through DNA computing, can be programmed (literally through lines of code) to include custom hallucinations
- The Mafia could traffic schematics for normal and custom framejacking brain augmentation

## Quantum Decryption

## DNA Encoding — Technical Notes

`let LEN = number of enzyme-steps in one chromosome`
`DNA encoded in AES-LEN`

This is the mathematical foundation of all DNA computing in the series. The length of the enzyme-step chain in a chromosome determines the AES key length.
