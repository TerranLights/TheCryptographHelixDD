The Siren
	- offers a total release from the limitations of his life
	- her presence is always heightened and sexually charged
	- transports the man to a realm of pure pleasure
	- takes control of the male libido by embodying his fantasy

The Ideal Lover
	- thrives on people's broken dreams, which become lifelong fantasies
	- is an artist in creating the illusion you require
	- wields limitless seductive power in a world of disappointment in people, events, reality
	- offers romance, adventure, and lofty spiritual communion

The Dandy
	- is more fluid than we are
	- creates her own persona
	- excites us because she cannot be categorized
	- hints at a freedom we want for ourselves
	- fashions her own physical image, which is always startling

The Natural
	- represents the golden paradise of childhood we are always consciously or unconsciously trying to re-create
	- embodies the longed-for qualities of childhood-spontaneity, sincerity, unpretentiousness
	- gets us caught up in her playful spirit
	- neutralizes people's defensiveness and infects them with helpless delight

The Coquette
	- basically a cocktease
	- delays satisfaction, holding the victim in thrall
	- orchestrates a back-and-forth movement between hope and frustration
	- baits with the promise of reward: the hope of physical pleasure, happiness, fame by association, and power

The Charmer
	- seduces without sex
	- is a consummate manipulator
	- masks her cleverness by creating a mood of pleasure and comfort
	- deflects attention from herself and focuses it on her target
	- she understands your spirit, feels your pain, and adapts to your moods
	- in her presence, you feel better about yourself

The Charismatic
	- is a presence that excites us
	- has an inner quality of self-confidence, sexual energy, sense of purpose, contentment that most people lack and want
	- heightens her charisma with a piercing gaze, fiery oratory, an air of mystery
	- radiates intensity while remaining detached

The Star
	- offers an escape from the harshness of life in fantasies and dreams
	- stands out from others through a distinctive and appealing style
	- makes us want to watch her
	- vague and ethereal, keeping her distance
	- lets us imagine more than is there
	- has a dreamlike quality that works on our unconscious
	- projects a glittering but elusive presence
