# Memes — Overview

## What They Are

In the Cryptograph Helix universe, memes are not simply viral images or jokes — they are **literal living entities**, encoded in and sustained by group consciousness. They communicate via Jungian subconscious symbols and, by the time the story begins, some are becoming self-aware.

> *"What is consciousness? The ability to define. A = B×"*

## The Theory of Meme Consciousness

Memes communicate via symbolism — not language, but Jungian archetypal symbols embedded in the subconscious. They "talk" to their human hosts through dreams, impulses, and intuitions, without the hosts knowing it.

When a meme's symbolic language conflicts with a human host's own natural subconscious psyche's symbolism, the host can suffer a **psychoschizoid break** — a complete fracturing of self when two symbol systems war inside a single mind.

Memes also communicate with each other via symbolism, invisible to humans.

**Key question they raise:** How much are humans already like robots/AI? If memes are conscious, what does that mean for the humans who carry them?

## Real-World Seed Idea

Memes as folk art: internet culture over the past few decades has produced a distinct contemporary folk art tradition. Memes fill the creativity vacuum left by the collapse of quality in academic "modern art" — the same role Swedish folk pottery and carpentry once played versus royal art.

Concrete example: Because of the Doge/Shiba Inu meme, Shiba Inu dogs have enjoyed genuine evolutionary success purely because humans found the concept funny. On the sole basis of human thoughts and jokes, another species' breeding population increased. Memes can have actual influence upon reality — and if you take that far enough, you get the Cryptograph Helix universe.

## The IoT Assassination of "Elon Musk"

"Elon Musk" (a stand-in for the tech oligarch archetype) catches on to the memes and tries to develop a framejack antidote to destroy them. His own memes notify the other memes, and they organize an IoT assassination — orchestrated to look like it was committed by someone carrying rival memes.

## The 3-Act Arc

**Act 1 — Disturbance:** Realizing the simultaneous separatedness and unity of humans.
- **Doorway 1:** Humans notice their self-awareness and try to "framejack" them back into non-conscious automatondom.

**Act 2:**
- **Mirror Moment:** Humans mostly succeed. It's down to only a few self-aware memes remaining.
- **Doorway 2:** Memes go full-assault and hijack humans like giant mech suits. The first human notices.

**Act 3:**
- **Final Battle:** Memes cause psychoschizoid breaks in any human who tries to eliminate them back to non-consciousness — but must do so from within the human, and thus go Kamikaze.
