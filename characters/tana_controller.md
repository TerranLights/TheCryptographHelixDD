## Background

<!-- Life history, origin, formative experiences -->

A Space Traffic Controller at a TANA site — the in-universe equivalent of
an Air Traffic Control tower, but for the Solar Positioning System's
heliocentric navigation grid (see `world/navigation.md`). Notices a lone
ship's bearing doesn't match what the *official* (stale-Almanac) charts
say should be there — the discrepancy between a frozen correction-offset
Almanac and the ship's actual position. Remarks on it — something like
*"Oh, that's odd. Just one ship"* — then returns to whatever she was doing.
This is the long-flagged "stale Navigational Almanac" plot beat from
`TODO.md`, now given a mechanism and a character — book placement (6 or 7)
still open.

**Cross-project connection:** the ship she notices is the one from *The
Not-Departure series* (Doll-Fi/media/Audiocasts) — a separate audio-drama
project set concurrently with this book, not a subplot of it. The ship is
Doc's, en route to Saturn, about to be destroyed once it crosses into
unregulated "dead space." This scene is the full extent of the two
projects' overlap — deliberately minor, not a crossover plot. See that
project's `Tepenian_Reinterpretation.md` §1 and
`structure/Dev-Road-Map/02-Blocking-Decisions.md` for the other side of
this connection.
