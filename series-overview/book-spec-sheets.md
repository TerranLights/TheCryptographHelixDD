# Book Spec Sheets

A single, at-a-glance guiding document for what each book actually *is* — the plainest possible statement of theme, place, and story, extrapolated from `per-book_breakdown.md` and cross-checked against every other established source (`themes-and-structure.md`, `premise.md`, `faction-motivations.md`, `grand-percentage-structure.md`, `plot/series-map.md`, `plot/parallel-plots/`, `world/locations.md`, `world/technology.md`, `reference/continuity-guidelines.md`). Nothing below is invented — it is the existing canon organized per book so it can be checked at a glance instead of assembled from a dozen files each time.

Use this as the first stop when starting work on a book; use the linked source files when a beat needs full detail.

> **Origin note:** the user originally conceived this entire series — not just Book 7 — roughly nine years ago, before the Tepenian universe existed. Every book's material below is the *current, Tepenian-reconciled* state, but treat any given piece of established lore as potentially a standalone-universe leftover that hasn't been fully checked against Tepenian continuity yet, not just Book 7 (whose own beat sheets carry an explicit unreconciled-content warning — see that section below). When in doubt on an older or unusually specific detail, it's worth asking whether it predates the Tepenian integration.

## Book 1 — *Terranocracy Anointed*

**Theme:** Privacy · **Antagonist:** The God Complex · **KWML:** Passive Shadow — King (Good Times) · **Monomyth/Harmon:** You

**Primary Locations:** Mars, Earth (also: Venus)

**The Core Idea:** Earthtech having control over the people of Mars — the series' opening statement of the surveillance-state premise everything else escalates from.

**What's in Motion:**
- The series opens on Venus's floating gulags — political prisoners mined as labor above the atmosphere. This is the opening image of the entire series and establishes Venus as the place of suffering that haunts every later book.
- **The Fibonacci Flower** is planted here as the series' Care Package/MacGuffin: an imprisoned father encodes an encrypted message into flower DNA and sends seeds to Mars. Decoded: *"They're killing us."* This is the single longest-running setup in the series — it doesn't pay off until Book 7's Q-Factor.
- Mafia Arc, Circle 1 (Privacy) opening steps: corporation/government hybrids (Earthtech) are using AI/ML surveillance across a partially-terraformed Mars; a handful of cryptographers and hackers, resenting their total lack of privacy, take the first steps toward what becomes DNA computing. This book is their "You" and "Need."
- The God Complex antagonist is textural here, not just structural: Earthtech's leadership are android mind-uploads of people who once considered themselves gods (`premise.md`).

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom begins passive — a King who doesn't yet know he's under siege. Good Times, about to end.

**Structural Notes:** Grand Opening Image (page 1 of 14,000) is, by definition, this book's own Opening Image. First of the two Privacy-arc books.

## Book 2 — *The Autonomy Allele*

**Theme:** Privacy · **Antagonist:** Technosurveillance · **KWML:** Passive Shadow — King → Magician (Good Times → Weak Men) · **Monomyth/Harmon:** Need

**Primary Locations:** Mars, Earth, Venus (also: starlifting the Sun, the Belt)

**The Core Idea:** Genetic Engineering and DNA Computation — the technology underpinning the entire series properly comes online.

**What's in Motion:**
- **László** is introduced around event 2.10 — on-page and observing well before his own Harmon cycle begins (Book 4, event 4.1).
- The **piano-key computer** — Earthtech's brain-wave decryption tool — is introduced: the direct technological expression of this book's Technosurveillance antagonist.
- Mafia Arc, Circle 1 continues: DNA-computing breakthroughs with plant DNA; Earth Law-Enforcement notices inconsistencies in its own data-mining and starts hunting the people responsible — the chase that eventually forges the Mafia into an actual "tribe of ideas."
- Starlifting the Sun is referenced as an active location/technology from this book onward.

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom recognizes something is wrong (Need) — the passive King starts becoming a Magician, someone who acts on hidden knowledge rather than open force.

**Structural Notes:** **Hinge Book.** The Grand Break into Two (20% of the entire 14-volume series) lands ~40% through this book — within ~200 pages of Book 2's own local Midpoint (its Vol 1/Vol 2 break). The series' grand commitment to its journey and this book's own Mirror Moment land in the same neighborhood. Second and final Privacy-arc book — the theme resolves as the series turns to Ethics.

## Book 3 — *Neurotropic Dreamtime*

**Theme:** Ethics · **Antagonist:** Degenerate Hedonism · **KWML:** Active Shadow — Magician (Weak Men) · **Monomyth/Harmon:** Go

**Primary Locations:** Mars, the Belt (also: Venus, starlifting the Sun; Jupiter infrastructure begins)

**The Core Idea:** Mafia life, drug addiction, framejacking, and e-thottery — the human cost of the world the Mafia has built.

**What's in Motion:**
- **Framejacking** — a brain-augmentation technique inducing subjective time dilation — becomes a major societal addiction problem here (`world/technology.md`). It burns out the brain with overuse and is trafficked via DNA-encoded schematics by the Russian Mafia. This is the book's Degenerate Hedonism antagonist made literal.
- This is roughly where Mafia Arc Circle 1 (Privacy) closes out — the Mafia is fully born as a "tribe of ideas," fights pitched battles with Earth Law-Enforcement, and begins building its first Jupiterian shellworld — and Circle 2 (Ethics, Books 3–5) opens: the first shellworld is now fully developed, DNA computing carries full schematics, and animal-DNA experimentation is underway alongside the framejacking-driven addiction crisis.
- The **Colombian E-thot**'s personal arc (`plot/parallel-plots/colombian-e-thot.md`) is active in this window — e-thottery is explicitly this book's topic, and her story (a Chinese-mafioso client, Russian brain-encryption, the IoT assassination she witnesses) threads through here and into Book 4.
- Jupiter infrastructure begins construction during this book's timeframe.

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom crosses the threshold into unfamiliar territory (Go) — leaving Privacy's relatively contained stakes for Ethics' messier, appetite-driven ones.

**Structural Notes:** The end of this book (~43% of the series) falls just before the Grand Midpoint — closing the Ethics arc's opening chapter right as the grand story approaches its structural center. First of three Ethics-arc books.

## Book 4 — *The Architect Awakes*

**Theme:** Ethics · **Antagonist:** Escapist Codependency · **KWML:** Active Shadow — Magician → Lover (Weak Men → Hard Times) · **Monomyth/Harmon:** Search

**Primary Locations:** Mars, Earth, the Belt (also: Earth-Moon-Sun LaGrange; Jupiter toward the end)

**The Core Idea:** The memes are alive and want self-determination.

**What's in Motion:**
- **This is the single most structurally important book in the series.** Book 4 Volume 1's final page is simultaneously this book's own Midpoint/Mirror Moment *and* the Grand Midpoint of the entire 14-volume series — a geometrically exact alignment (`grand-percentage-structure.md` calls it "the mathematical heart of the series"). Whatever happens on that page has to carry double weight: it answers "Who is Freedom? What has Freedom become? What must Freedom become?" for both Book 4 and the entire series at once.
- **László's personal Harmon cycle begins at event 4.1.**
- **Memes come online at ~event 4.7** — the Meme Nation arc (`plot/parallel-plots/meme-nation.md`) begins: the memes realize they're self-aware but disconnected from one another and want to unite.
- Mafia Arc Circle 2 (Ethics) mid-stretch: anti-Mafia resistance cells form — and, ironically, curdle into becoming Mafias themselves; the Mafias move into human-DNA customs-evasion; epigenetic disorders begin surfacing in people "born" of DNA-cryptography experiments.

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom's Road of Trials (Search) — the KWML shift from Magician to Lover begins here, as detached manipulation of hidden systems gives way to entanglement and attachment (mirrored by the memes' own longing to unite).

**Structural Notes:** **Hinge Book — exact.** This is the series' center of gravity. Second of three Ethics-arc books.

## Book 5 — *Eclipse of a Ganymede Sunrise*

**Theme:** Ethics · **Antagonist:** The Chaos of Anarchy · **KWML:** Active Shadow — Lover → Warrior (Hard Times → Strong Men) · **Monomyth/Harmon:** Find

**Primary Locations:** Jupiter (also: Saturn)

**The Core Idea:** Isolationism and the ethics of lawlessness in the Jovian metasystem.

> **Title flag:** `per-book_breakdown.md` lists this book's tentative title as *"Rays of a Ganymede Sunrise,"* while every other source in the repo (`title-candidates.md`, this book's own `overview.md`, `plot/series-map.md`) has *"Eclipse of a Ganymede Sunrise"* as the current ✓ pick. Per the user (2026-07-12): genuinely undecided between the two, leaning "Eclipse" but open to "Rays." Using "Eclipse" here as the working title; not yet locked.

**What's in Motion:**
- All Jovian shellworlds are fully functional; Saturn mining bases (Titan, etc.) are now established. The outer solar system's Mafia-run society is at its most expansive and least governed — the Chaos of Anarchy made geographic.
- Mafia Arc Circle 2 (Ethics) closes here: an all-out inter-Mafia war, plus Mafia/Law-Enforcement warfare, erupts; Earth banishes the Mafias to the outer worlds, effectively turning their own shellworlds into prison-worlds; the different Mafias negotiate a shaky, fragile truce to end the book. **That ceasefire breaks catastrophically in Book 6.**

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom finds what it needs, but not as expected (Find) — the KWML pivot from Lover to Warrior begins as the fragile peace fails to hold.

**Structural Notes:** The end of this book (~71% of the series) lands right before the Grand All Is Lost — the moment the Ethics theme resolves is also the moment the grand arc begins its steepest descent. Third and final Ethics-arc book.

## Book 6 — *Storm over Callisto*

**Theme:** Power · **Antagonist:** Powerlust · **KWML:** Light/Completion — Warrior (Strong Men); also noted Magician → Lover · **Monomyth/Harmon:** Take/Pay

**Primary Locations:** Jupiter, Mars, the Belt (also: starlifters — DAMRAGE cult)

**The Core Idea:** Out-and-open Mafia warfare; the Resistance trying to check the Mafias' power.

**What's in Motion:**
- The fragile Book 5 ceasefire breaks irreparably: a mining-transport accident cascades into cross-faction hostage-taking and a full inter-Mafia war — Mafia Arc Circle 3 (Power)'s opening steps.
- **Earthtech launches a full-scale assault on all the Mafias** — the series-level Doorway of No Return #2 (`plot/series-map.md`).
- The **DAMRAGE cult** reappears in the starlifter context.
- The Mafia arc's most emotionally central beat lands here: an underling is ordered to kill a friend (does it, reluctantly), then ordered to kill his own mother (can't do it — kills his Mafia bosses instead), then burns his brain out on maxed-out framejacking to get her to safety.

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom pays a heavy price (Take/Pay) — Light/Completion begins here, but at enormous cost.

**Structural Notes:** **Hinge Book — the darkest-then-decisive book of the entire series.** Contains both the Grand All Is Lost (~75%, landing near this book's own local Break into Two) and the Grand Break into Three (~80%, landing near this book's own local Midpoint) — only ~700 pages apart. This book's own Mirror Moment falls inside the Grand Dark Night of the Soul. First of the two Power-arc books.

## Book 7 — *Embers of a Frostbitten Star*

**Theme:** Power · **Antagonist:** Human Extinction · **KWML:** Light/Completion — Warrior (Strong Men → Good Times) · **Monomyth/Harmon:** Return → Change

**Primary Locations:** Mars, Earth (also: Venus, the Belt, the starlifters) — *this fills in what this book's own `overview.md` still lists as "TBD," sourced directly from `per-book_breakdown.md`.*

**The Core Idea:** Earthtech, the various Mafias, and the Resistance all against each other — a total power struggle resolved by humanity's dispersal across the solar system.

**What's in Motion:**
- Mafia Arc Circle 3 closes: resistance cells — having killed much of the old Mafia leadership — disband and build an actual system of self-government with a constitution, declaring the outer worlds fully independent.
- Meme Nation arc closes: after their aggressive push for independence nearly gets their human hosts (and thus themselves) killed, the memes choose symbiosis over sovereignty. They don't get what they want (their own nation) but they get what they need — an understanding of human nature.
- **The Final Battle:** humans integrate their memes (their own collective Shadow); convince the memes to integrate their own Shadow in turn; together they face Earthtech — too egotistical (Satanic, in the Jungian sense used by `themes-and-structure.md`) to ever acknowledge having a Shadow at all.
- **The Fibonacci Flower returns as the Q-Factor**, closing the loop opened by Book 1's Care Package.
- **Known ending:** humanity disperses into distinct population streams — some return to Earth (Hungarians/Altay Nation leading), some remain on Mars, a substantial population colonizes Jupiter (seeding what becomes the Outer Tepenia 1 civilization ~1,000 years later), and some are in transit toward Saturn. This dispersal *is* the defeat of the Human Extinction antagonist: humanity can't be exterminated while it occupies four distinct points in the solar system.

**Where This Sits in Freedom's Arc:** Freedom returns transformed (Return/Change) — Strong Men gives way to Good Times, closing the KWML cycle where Book 1 began.

**Structural Notes:** Grand Closing Image (page 14,000) is, by definition, this book's own Closing Image — mirroring Book 1's identical alignment at the opening. Second and final Power-arc book.

> **Inherited flag** (from this book's own `overview.md`): much of the existing beat-sheet content for Book 7 was developed for a standalone (pre-Tepenian) universe roughly nine years ago and has not been rethought for the current Tepenian-universe context. Treat existing beat sheet files as rough starting material only, not settled structure.
