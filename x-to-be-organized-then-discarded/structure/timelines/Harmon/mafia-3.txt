CENTRAL THEME:
	Power

1. YOU		// THE STATUS QUO // ZONE OF FAMILIARITY

	All Jupiterian shellworlds are fully functional

	There are also now mining bases on Titan and other of Saturn's moons

2. NEED		// SOMETHING ISN'T RIGHT // THEY WANT SOMETHING

	Each Mafia wants control over all the outer-worlds

3. GO		// CROSS THE THRESHOLD // EMBARK UPON THE JOURNEY // UNFAMILIAR SITUATION

	During a mining transportation mission, one mafioso accidentally kills another rival mafioso

	One mafia that hadn't been affected believed the target was one of their guys.

	This 3rd Mafia kills one of the accidentally-firing mafia's members in retaliation

	Every side ends up taking hostages from other sides

	The shaky, fragile ceasefire is now fully and irreparably broken. This results in another inter-Mafia war

4. SEARCH	// MAIN TENSION // ROAD OF TRIALS // ADAPT TO THE SITUATION

	The common people have had enough and work to put an end to Mafia rule.

	(cf. Irish resistance, "The Good Mothers" book, etc)

5. FIND		// FIND WHAT YOU NEED, BUT IT DOESN'T WORK OUT AS YOU EXPECTED // MEETING THE GODDESS

	Two of the resistance who'd killed much of the Mafia that the bosses worked to track down are found

	They turn out to be an old friend and the mother of one of the "underlings"

	He's ordered to first kill his friend, which he reluctantly does

	He's then ordered to kill his own mother. He can't do it, and kills his own mafia bosses

6. PAY		// PAY A HEAVY PRICE

	The entire Mafia are now after him, and he must get his mom to safety

	The only way he can have enough time to strategize a play-by-play is to use his own framejacking drugs

	He "dials his drugs up to 11"

7. RETURN	// RETURN, BUT THERE'S A TWIST // BRINGING IT HOME

	He succeeds in getting his mother to safety, but his brain turns to jelly from the heavy drugs

8. CHANGE	// NOW A NEW PERSON // MASTER OF BOTH WORLDS

	The resistance cells disband and try to work out an actual system of self-government with a constitution

	Declare the outer-worlds fully independent and begin rebuilding their lives
