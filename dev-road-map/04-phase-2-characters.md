## Character Development Standard

A character is Phase 2 complete when the file contains:
- Voice sample (a representative quote or internal thought)
- Core contradiction (what they want vs. what they need)
- KWML archetype for their arc
- Harmon circle (if not already in `plot/parallel-plots/`)
- 48-laws mapping (which they follow, which they break, and why)
- Per-book role from first appearance to last
