# Universe Timeline

The Cryptograph Helix is the sequel era to **Inner Tepenia** (~2083–2827). Both are set in the same universe.

> The old notes say "2279 A.D." — **this date is outdated/placeholder.** The correct era is approximately the early 3000s–3100s (deliberately fluid; do not lock a specific year until world-building context demands it).

## How Inner Tepenia Leads Into Cryptograph Helix

**The off-world evacuees → the Solar System civilization of the 3000s–3100s**
The people who escaped Tepenia via Amundsen Tower built orbital stations, Phobos/Deimos infrastructure, and Mars colonization. By the Cryptograph Helix era, their descendants form the solar system civilization — including the Martian colonies with Hungarian city names.

> **Their founding trauma is documented at `world/orbital-origins.md`.** Orbit *watched* the Long Night War and was helpless to intervene — witnessing its progenitive (or sister) civilization die without being able to reach it. That file also records the pre-2812 archive inheritance (orbit received Arcanet backups carrying salvaged Upper Earth and pre-war human internet data, so the solar civilization did not start from cultural scratch), and why this population are **witnesses rather than survivors** — a different wound from the one surface Tepenians carry, producing a different people. Handed off from the Inner Tepenia GDD repo, 2026-08-29.

**Upper Earth powers → Earthtech**
The anti-robot Upper Earth nations that waged both anti-robot wars evolve over roughly 200–300 years into the Silicon Valley tech-giant government of the Cryptograph Helix era. The surveillance-state tendencies are inherited.

**Robot sentience (2083 onward) → meme sentience**
Robot consciousness is settled ancient history by the 3000s. The Cryptograph Helix introduces a new form of emergent consciousness — memes evolving into sentient entities — as the next philosophical frontier.

**Gyeong-ja Yun (2318)** — robot DJ whose case triggered robot personhood — is directly resonant with the Cryptograph Helix's mafia/e-thot dynamics. Ancient history by the 3000s but thematically foundational.

**China's collapse** — referenced in Cryptograph Helix backstory — lines up with the Sinian Federation already existing by 2318 in Inner Tepenia as a post-collapse reconstruction.

**Venus** — floating gulags in the Cryptograph Helix; Inner Tepenia's off-world evacuees were already "moving toward Venus" during the Second Interwar Period. The ~200–300 year gap (post-Long Night War to Cryptograph Helix's opening) accounts for Venus becoming colonized and then exploited.

## Lore Constraints for Cryptograph Helix (~3000s–3100s)

- First sentient robot: 2083. By the 3000s, this is ~1,000 years of settled history.
- Robot personhood ruling: April 27, 2318 (Jeju-do). Ancient history.
- Falkland Treaty: June 21, 2564. ~450–550 years before Cryptograph Helix begins.
- Long Night War: ~2812 (mid-to-late) — corrected 2026-07-11 from a previously assumed "early 2700s," per the official Tepenian Universe Timeline repo (github.com/TerranLights/TepenianUniverseTimeline). ~200–300 years before Cryptograph Helix.
- Sinian Federation (post-China) already exists by 2318 — consistent with China having collapsed long before the 3000s.
- Jupiter colonization happens *during* Cryptograph Helix events. At the series' opening, only Mars, its moons, and limited inner solar system regions are colonized.
