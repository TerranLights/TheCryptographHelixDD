# Parallel Plot — The DNA Mafias (3-Circle Arc)

Three Dan Harmon Story Circles across the Mafia faction's full arc.

---

## Circle 1 — Privacy (Books 1–2)

**Central Theme: Privacy**

**1. You** — The Status Quo
- Corporation/government hybrids are using AI, Machine Learning, and Neural Networks to spy on everybody
- The setting is on Mars: extensively developed, somewhat partially terraformed

**2. Need** — Something Isn't Right
- A few tech-savvy cryptographers/hackers hate the fact that they have no personal freedom

**3. Go** — Cross the Threshold
- They decide to do something about it and start experimenting with DNA computing

**4. Search** — Road of Trials
- They achieve a series of breakthroughs with plant DNA
- Earth Law-Enforcement catches wind of this when they notice inconsistencies in their data-mining
- Earth Law-Enforcement is now after them

**5. Find** — Meeting the Goddess
- This is where the Mafia is born; they are abandoned by their families and form a "tribe of ideas"

**6. Pay** — Pay a Heavy Price
- There are huge battles on Mars and in space with Earth Law-Enforcement

**7. Return** — Return, But There's a Twist
- They begin building their own shellworld planet in orbit around Jupiter to live unperturbed
- Set up mining bases on Jupiter's moons

**8. Change** — Master of Both Worlds
- On their Jupiterian shellworld, they're free to run data trafficking and have complete power and authority
- Earth Law-Enforcement notices spectral anomalies coming from Jupiter and decides to investigate

## Circle 2 — Ethics (Books 3–5)

**Central Theme: Ethics**

**1. You** — The Status Quo
- The first Jupiterian shellworld is now fully developed
- DNA Computing is refined to the point of carrying full schematics
- The Mafia has already moved up to experimenting with animal DNA

**2. Need** — Something Isn't Right
- People are paying the Mafia for framejacking drugs, and there's a huge addiction problem
- Some wonder if the toll on people's minds and the breakdown of families is justified

**3. Go** — Cross the Threshold
- Small yet noticeable anti-Mafia sentiment is beginning to grow
- Resistance cells take shape and try to take the Mafia down
- Some begin collaborating with Earth Law-Enforcement, spilling information, asking for help

**4. Search** — Road of Trials
- Some figure out how to defraud the Mafia out of their money and build their own shellworld moons
- The original mafiosos try to find out who's doing it (because it's hard to miss a new moon)
- All sides figure out newer and better weaponry and drugs; all sides begin selling personal data

**5. Find** — Meeting the Goddess
- What started as anti-Mafia resistance cells end up becoming Mafias themselves
- The framejacking drug-addiction problem has gotten much worse
- The DNA Mafias are now experimenting with human DNA to pass actual people through customs
- Mafias infiltrate Earth and crash the Machine Learning AI systems so that people can live freely

**6. Pay** — Pay a Heavy Price
- An all-out inter-Mafia war (as well as Mafia/Law-Enforcement warfare) erupts; many die on all sides
- Humans born of DNA-cryptography experiments are now suffering severe epigenetic disorders
- Earth banishes all Mafias to the outer worlds; uses their planetary habitats as prison worlds

**7. Return** — Return, But There's a Twist
- The different Mafias work out a shaky, fragile truce/ceasefire

**8. Change** — Master of Both Worlds
- Many of the prisoners being sent out know the Mafias from Circle 1; their families were killed by the Mafia
- Prisoner-exilees seek revenge upon the Mafias who killed their families

---

## Circle 3 — Power (Books 6–7)

**Central Theme: Power**

**1. You** — The Status Quo
- All Jupiterian shellworlds are fully functional
- There are also now mining bases on Titan and other Saturnian moons

**2. Need** — Something Isn't Right
- Each Mafia wants control over all the outer worlds

**3. Go** — Cross the Threshold
- During a mining transportation mission, one mafioso accidentally kills another rival mafioso
- A third Mafia that wasn't involved believed the target was one of their guys
- This third Mafia kills one of the accidentally-firing Mafia's members in retaliation
- Every side ends up taking hostages from other sides
- The shaky, fragile ceasefire is now fully and irreparably broken. Another inter-Mafia war erupts.

**4. Search** — Road of Trials
- The common people have had enough and work to put an end to Mafia rule
- (cf. Irish resistance, "The Good Mothers" etc.)

**5. Find** — Meeting the Goddess
- Two resistance members who'd killed much of the Mafia leadership are tracked down
- They turn out to be an old friend and the mother of one of the "underlings"
- He is ordered to first kill his friend, which he reluctantly does
- He is then ordered to kill his own mother. He can't do it. He kills his own Mafia bosses instead.

**6. Pay** — Pay a Heavy Price
- The entire Mafia is now after him; he must get his mother to safety
- The only way to have enough time to strategize is to use his own framejacking drugs
- He "dials his drugs up to 11"

**7. Return** — Return, But There's a Twist
- He succeeds in getting his mother to safety, but his brain turns to jelly from the heavy drugs

**8. Change** — Master of Both Worlds
- The resistance cells disband and try to work out an actual system of self-government with a constitution
- Declare the outer worlds fully independent and begin rebuilding their lives
