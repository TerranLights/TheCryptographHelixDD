# Grand Percentage Structure — Series-Wide Beat Alignment

The entire 14-volume series mapped as a single story using the Save the Cat percentage anchors, then cross-referenced against the localized beat structure of each individual book. Use this when planning story components to identify where grand-arc beats and book-level beats land on the same page — those are the moments that carry double structural weight.

## Grand Percentage Structure

The entire series treated as one ~14,000-page story (base: 1,000 pages per volume × 14 volumes). The math is structural — all alignments hold at any uniform volume length (1,000pp or 1,100pp or any other).

| Grand Beat | % | Absolute Page | Falls In |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening Image | 0% | 1 | Book 1, Vol 1 — opening |
| Theme Stated | 5% | 700 | Book 1, Vol 1 — p.700 |
| Catalyst | 10% | 1,400 | Book 1, Vol 2 — p.400 |
| Break into Two | 20% | 2,800 | Book 2, Vol 1 — p.800 |
| Midpoint | 50% | 7,000 | Book 4, Vol 1 — **final page** |
| All Is Lost | 75% | 10,500 | Book 6, Vol 1 — p.500 |
| Break into Three | 80% | 11,200 | Book 6, Vol 2 — p.200 |

**Zone spans:**

## Local Percentage Structure

Identical for every book. Each book = 2,000 pages (Vol 1 = pp.1–1,000, Vol 2 = pp.1,001–2,000).

| Local Beat | % | Page within book | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening Image | 0% | 1 | Vol 1, opening |
| Theme Stated | 5% | 100 | Vol 1, p.100 |
| Catalyst | 10% | 200 | Vol 1, p.200 |
| Break into Two | 20% | 400 | Vol 1, p.400 |
| Midpoint | 50% | 1,000 | Vol 1, **final page** — always |
| All Is Lost | 75% | 1,500 | Vol 2, p.500 |

| Closing Image | 100% | 2,000 | Vol 2, final page |

The Midpoint always falls at the exact Vol 1 / Vol 2 break — this is a structural law of the series, not a coincidence.

## The Alignments

### Exact Alignments

**Grand Opening Image = Local Opening Image of Book 1**
Page 1. Structurally inevitable.

**Grand Midpoint = Local Midpoint of Book 4 = the Vol 1 / Vol 2 break of Book 4**
Page 7,000. Geometrically exact — holds at any uniform volume length. This is not a coincidence of page count; it is a structural truth built into the series' architecture.

The grand series' Mirror Moment for Freedom ("Who is Freedom? What has Freedom become? What must Freedom become?") falls at exactly the same page as Book 4's own Mirror Moment. The deepest question of the entire 14,000-page arc coincides with the deepest question of the middle book's first half. **This is the mathematical heart of the series.** Whatever happens at Book 4, Vol 1's final pages needs to carry double weight — it is simultaneously the Mirror Moment of Book 4 individually and the Mirror Moment of Freedom's entire grand journey.

**Grand Closing Image = Local Closing Image of Book 7**
Page 14,000. Structurally inevitable.

---

### Near-Exact Alignments (~100–200 pages / 0.1–0.2 volumes)

**Grand All Is Lost ≈ Local Break into Two of Book 6**
- Grand All Is Lost: p.10,500 (75% of series)
- Local Break into Two of Book 6: p.10,400 (20% into Book 6)
- Gap: ~100 pages

Freedom's absolute lowest point in the grand arc — the moment when all hope seems gone — falls almost exactly as Book 6 crosses its own threshold and commits to something it cannot undo. The grand nadir and Book 6's local point-of-no-return are essentially simultaneous.

**Grand Break into Three ≈ Local Midpoint of Book 6 (= end of Book 6, Vol 1)**
- Grand Break into Three: p.11,200 (80% of series)
- Local Midpoint of Book 6: p.11,000 (end of Book 6, Vol 1)
- Gap: ~200 pages

Freedom's grand moment of clarity — understanding what it must do and who it must become — arrives almost exactly at Book 6's own Mirror Moment. Grand clarity and local self-confrontation land in the same neighborhood.

**Consequence: Book 6 is the darkest-then-decisive book of the entire series.** It contains both the Grand All Is Lost and the Grand Break into Three within its 2,000 pages, with only ~700 pages between them. The series-level lowest point and the series-level turning point both happen inside Book 6.

**Grand Break into Two ≈ Local Midpoint of Book 2**
- Grand Break into Two: p.2,800 (20% of series)
- Local Midpoint of Book 2: p.3,000 (end of Book 2, Vol 1)
- Gap: ~200 pages

Freedom's grand commitment to the journey (Break into Two — the door that closes behind it, after which the old rules no longer apply) falls just as Book 2 is hitting its own Mirror Moment. The grand story "begins in earnest" at almost the same moment Book 2 is confronting its most pivotal internal question.

### Other Notable Observations

**The Grand Catalyst (p.1,400) falls at 70% through Book 1 — Local Bad Guys Close In territory.**
The event that sets the entire 14-book journey in motion arrives when Book 1 is already in its darkest stretch locally. The grand call to adventure and Book 1's local crisis of mounting pressure overlap.

**The end of Book 3 (p.6,000) falls at ~43% of the series — just before the Grand Midpoint.**
Book 3 closes the Ethics arc's opening chapter right as the grand story is approaching its halfway Mirror Moment. The transition out of Book 3 into Book 4 is the approach to the series' structural center.

**The end of Book 5 (p.10,000) falls at ~71% of the series — approaching Grand All Is Lost.**
Book 5 closes the Ethics arc AND lands right before the grand series' lowest point. The moment the Ethics theme resolves is also the moment the grand arc begins its steepest descent.

**Book 6's Local Midpoint (p.11,000) falls at 78.6% of the series — inside Grand Dark Night of the Soul.**
Book 6's Mirror Moment ("Who is Freedom now? What must it become?") is embedded inside the grand series' darkest hour. The local self-confrontation and the grand darkness are the same thing.
