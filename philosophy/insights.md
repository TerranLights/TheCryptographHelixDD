## On Memes as Folk Art

Considering the fact that, over the course of the past couple of decades, a particular, distinct internet culture has arisen and continues to take shape, memes are now the contemporary form of Folk Art. This may be one of the reasons why they're so popular.

There is a huge difference between the pottery, carpentry, etc., produced by common day-to-day working people and the art produced by royalty — this has always been true regardless of what society you're using as a reference. Nowadays, with the advent of so-called "Modern Art" (which fills a creativity vacuum from a total lack of quality in Academia), memes further fill this vacuum since we no longer have actual, literal royalty.

## On Memes Having Real-World Influence

A realization: there is now a growing market and financial incentive for dog-breeders cultivating Shiba Inu dogs, specifically because of the "Doge" meme. On the sole basis of humans' thoughts, jokes, and ideas, another species has enjoyed evolutionary success — simply because we like the concept. Memes can have actual influence upon reality.

This is the seed of the universe: taken far enough, memes are literally alive.

## On Consciousness

What is consciousness?

> *The ability to define. A = B×*

If we are not different from robots in any meaningful way (i.e., if there is no physical soul), then our consciousness is only our brain circuitry. And if so:
- What might be the physics-based model of the soul?
- Does it matter that we're based on DNA?
- If DNA plays a role in the existence of the soul, what does that mean for the genetically engineered people created purely for data trafficking?

**Mind-upload philosophy:**
- Mind-upload is not "cut-paste" — it's "copy-paste"
- The original dies; the copy lives on
- If no physical soul exists: two separate consciousnesses
- If a physical soul exists: the organic body becomes inert upon upload

The very common trope of "how robots can become like humans" almost never touches the inverse question: in what ways are humans already like robots? This question makes people extremely uncomfortable. It is one of the series' central provocations.

## On Meme Consciousness and Communication

Memes communicate via Jungian subconscious symbols (Jungian Depth Psychology). They talk to their human hosts through the subconscious.

When a meme's symbol language conflicts with a human host's own natural subconscious psyche's symbol language, the conflict can be so strong that the host suffers a **psychoschizoid break**.

Memes also communicate with each other via symbolism — invisible to humans.

This calls into question: how much are humans already like robots/AI?
