CENTRAL THEME:
	Ethics

1. YOU		// THE STATUS QUO // ZONE OF FAMILIARITY

	The first Jupiterian shellworld is now fully developed.

	DNA Computing is refined to the point of carrying full schematics

	The Mafia has already moved up to experimenting with animal DNA

2. NEED		// SOMETHING ISN'T RIGHT // THEY WANT SOMETHING

	People are paying the Mafia for framejacking drugs, and there's a huge addiction problem

	Some wonder if the toll on people's minds and the breakdown of families is justified

3. GO		// CROSS THE THRESHOLD // EMBARK UPON THE JOURNEY // UNFAMILIAR SITUATION

	Small yet noticeable anti-Mafia sentiment is beginning to grow

	Resistance cells take shape and try to take the Mafia down

	Some begin collaborating with Earth Law-Enforcement, spilling information, and ask for help

4. SEARCH	// MAIN TENSION // ROAD OF TRIALS // ADAPT TO THE SITUATION

	Some figure out how to defraud the Mafia out of their money and build their own shellworld moons

	The original mafiosos try to find out who's doing it (because it's hard to miss a new moon)

	They all figure out newer and better weaponry and drugs, and all sides begin selling personal data

5. FIND		// FIND WHAT YOU NEED, BUT IT DOESN'T WORK OUT AS YOU EXPECTED // MEETING THE GODDESS

	What started as anti-Mafia resistance cells end up becoming Mafias themselves

	The framejacking drug-addiction problem has gotten much worse

	The DNA Mafias are now experimenting with human DNA in order to pass actual people through customs

	Mafias infiltrate Earth and crash the Machine Learning AI systems so that people can live freely

6. PAY		// PAY A HEAVY PRICE

	An all-out inter-Mafia war (as well as Mafia/Law-Enforcement warfare) erupts; many people on all sides die

	Humans who'd been born of DNA-cryptography experiments are now suffering severe epigenetic disorders

	Earth banishes all Mafias to the outer worlds, and uses their planetary habitats as prison worlds

7. RETURN	// RETURN, BUT THERE'S A TWIST // BRINGING IT HOME

	The different Mafias work out a shaky, fragile truce/ceasefire

8. CHANGE	// NOW A NEW PERSON // MASTER OF BOTH WORLDS

	Many of the prisoners being sent out know the Mafias from book one; their families were killed by the Mafia

	Prisoner-exilees seek revenge upon the Mafias who killed their families
