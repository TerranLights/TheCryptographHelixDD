# Chinese

## Overview

China rose to near-global dominance in the 2200s and then collapsed before the Cryptograph Helix story began, primarily due to catastrophic demographic decline. A century of the One-Child Policy, followed by accelerating hypergamy, meant China couldn't sustain replacement birthrates. They conquered a global empire and then faded away before the story opens.

American-Chinese space settlements were built side-by-side during China's peak expansion. When China faded, the Silicon Valley collective took their place as the dominant power.

Note: The **Sinian Federation** — a post-collapse reconstruction of China — already existed in the Inner Tepenia era (~2318). This is consistent with China having collapsed and partially reformed before the Cryptograph Helix era.

## Role in the Story

The **Chinese Mafia** is one of the five major mafia factions — see `factions/mafias/chinese/overview.md`.
