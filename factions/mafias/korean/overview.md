# Korean Mafia — Overview

## Identity

Korean descendants of the Tepenian exile wave carry a specific cultural inheritance: Unified Korea hosted the Jeju-do International Court ruling of April 27, 2318 — the judgment that established full legal personhood for robots. That ruling is ancient history by the ~3000s, but it is foundational history. Korean culture in the off-world civilization has a particular relationship to questions of personhood, legal systems, and the boundary between rights and exploitation.

How that inheritance shaped a criminal organization — and what kind of criminal organization it produced — is to be developed.
