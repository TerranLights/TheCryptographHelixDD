# Technology

## DNA Computing

Data is encoded into DNA rather than binary electrical impulses. This is the series' core technological conceit.

Encoding method: `let LEN = number of enzyme-steps in one chromosome; DNA encoded in AES-LEN`

Data can be broken into blocks spread across multiple organisms — plants, animals, humans — to hide tracks and evade detection.

## Framejacking

A brain-augmentation technique that induces time dilation: the user's subjective experience slows dramatically while the outside world continues at normal speed.

**Uses:**
- Fighting multiple attackers simultaneously
- Processing complex decisions that must be made within minutes
- Extended subjective time for problem-solving
- Feeling an orgasm that lasts subjectively for months
- Winning competitive gaming tournaments

**Dangers:**
- Burns the brain out if used for too long — literally turns the brain to jelly
- Due to its highly neurotropic properties, it is extremely addictive
- Societal scourge of addiction becomes a major plot theme

**Distribution:** Schematics for do-it-yourself framejacking (including custom hallucination programming) are DNA-encoded and trafficked by the Russian Mafia.

*(Note: "Framejacking" was coined by Dennis E. Taylor — the in-universe term may need to differ. Consider renaming.)*

## Brain Encryption

**Biological "private key" system** developed by the Chinese Mafia: brainwave interfaces using the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) and the tuberomamillary nucleus (TMN) as biological clock rhythms to generate a unique personal encryption key (STN = SCN/TMN system).

- The SCN is a subportion of the hypothalamus serving as the brain's "master clock"
- The TMN is a localized "sleep clock"
- Together they produce a key unique to each individual's biological rhythms

**Russian counter-hack:** The Russians figured out how to "hack" the SCN/TMN private key using meme-warfare, rendering Chinese mafiosos brainfucked by disrupting their biological clocks.

## Waveform Hashing (Russian Mafia)

The Russian Mafia uses waveforms as analogues to SHA256SUM — a low-tech, primitive security system deliberately chosen to protect against digital hacking. Techno-Primitivism as a security philosophy.

## Piano-Key Computer

Earthtech's brain-wave decryption algorithm. Brain waves are used to decode encrypted communications. The "piano-key" metaphor refers to the mapping of wave frequencies to readable data, the way piano keys map to musical notes.

Used by Earthtech to monitor Martian citizens through chips and IoT/local spying — identifying individuals, recording their physical histories, and decrypting their communications.

## Psychological Cell Algorithm

Earthtech's surveillance system: a series of consciousnesses modeled as either on or off. The state-value can change, but the algorithm that evokes those changes is unique to each individual. Algorithms of minds get compared against each other to measure a localized cluster-mind network meme — a living organism in itself. Ideas spread among sub-memes easily because there are no mathematical conflicts with the universe, forming a macro-meme that has evolved to survive.

## Meme Consciousness

What is consciousness? *The ability to define.* `A = B×`

Memes communicate via symbolism — specifically Jungian subconscious symbols. When a meme's symbolic language conflicts with a human host's natural subconscious symbolism, the host can suffer a **psychoschizoid break**.

Memes also communicate with each other via symbolism, invisible to humans.

Memes can have actual influence on reality. Example (from the present day): the Doge/Shiba Inu meme created a genuine breeding market for Shiba Inu dogs — a species enjoyed evolutionary success solely because humans found the meme funny.

## Mind Upload

Earthtech's "immortality" technique: mind-uploading into android bodies. The philosophy:

- Mind-upload is not "cut-paste" — it is "copy-paste"
- The original organic body dies; the copy lives on
- If no physical soul exists: two separate consciousnesses — the original ends, the copy begins
- If a physical soul exists: upon upload, the organic body becomes inert; the soul transfers into the machine

This calls into question the nature of identity, continuity of consciousness, and the soul. Earthtech's android leadership are copies of people who once considered themselves gods.

## Blood Network

A healthcare system: upload blood to a computer network for analysis. Receive a patient file number. Use this number to book doctor's appointments and access your medical history.

## DNA-Based Customs Evasion

The Mafias move up to encoding actual people through customs using human DNA cryptography. People "born" of DNA-cryptography experiments are later found to suffer severe epigenetic disorders.
