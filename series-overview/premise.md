# Series Premise

## Core Concept

A society across Mars, its moons, and eventually the outer solar system has figured out how to encode data into DNA, rather than using binary electrical impulses. This DNA-based computation opens new frontiers — and new crimes.

A "DNA Mafia" forms. They begin by encoding data into the DNA of livestock, then progress to encoding it into humans, calling into question the ethics of the entire enterprise. Through DNA-encrypted schematics they traffic framejacking drugs, weapons designs, and personal data — all invisible to conventional surveillance.

## The Antagonist Power

In this era, the Silicon Valley tech giants have become the planetary government. San Francisco is the capital of Earth. Countries and governments technically exist, but they are a formality — the tech conglomerate is the real power. They run a surveillance state through a "psychological cell algorithm" and a "piano-key computer" brain-wave decryption system, mining citizens' thoughts and histories.

They are the carnal embodiment of narcissism. Their leadership are high-level androids with mind-uploads of people who once considered themselves gods — a crude attempt at immortality. (Note: "Earthtech" is the current placeholder name; see `reference/future-ideas.md` for renaming candidates.)

## The Mafia's Origin

The Mafia began as a ragtag team of hackers who encrypted, hashed, and obfuscated people's personal information — search history, purchase history — to protect common people from being spied on. They started charging for their services, then carved out a profitable niche. Their power grew. They began trafficking data.

Eventually they built entire shellworld mini-planets around Jupiter. First they trafficked data via plant DNA, then animal DNA, then human DNA. They broke sensitive code into blocks spread across multiple organisms to hide their tracks. Some within the Mafia began questioning the ethics of what they were doing.

One of the common traits of the origins of mafias throughout history is that they coalesced within the context of oppressive powers, banding together to protect themselves — and often, to protect other people. The story is ultimately a struggle between the Mafia and actual, genuine Fascism.

## The Central Conflict

> **Freedom versus Evolutionary Pressure**
> or, in simpler terms: **Man vs. Nature**

The "Mafia" is man's hubris and arrogance. "DNA" is nature. Man tries to control nature. Nature does not necessarily cooperate.

Philip K. Dick-style: **Freedom** is the main character.

## Honest Men

Mafiosos are honest because there are life-and-death consequences for crossing people. In government, politicians face absolutely no such consequences or retribution for crossing others. This creates a dark irony: the criminals operate with more integrity than the law.

## The Economy of Purpose

An economy is essentially run on a sense of purpose. Suicide is a symptom of a lack of purpose — economies are actually driven by purpose. This philosophical undercurrent runs through the series.

## World History (Brief)

In the 2200s, China had expanded to near-global dominance, but their dwindling birthrates — an accelerating consequence of the One-Child Policy and hypergamy — meant they couldn't hold the line demographically. The numbers crashed. China conquered a global empire and then faded away before the story began. American-Chinese space settlements were built side-by-side, and the "New Silicon Valley" collective took China's place as the dominant power. Countries still exist as fronts; "elections" are still a formality.
