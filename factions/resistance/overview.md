# The Resistance — Overview

## Who They Are

The Resistance is composed primarily of **Hungarians**, with a coalition that also includes **Mongolians** and **Kazakhs**. Outsiders call this alliance "the **Altay Nation**" — the Resistance never actually calls itself that or thinks of itself as formally unified. They're more of a loose confederation than a single entity.

They are the primary protagonists of the series. Their arc is the arc of **Freedom** itself.

## Where They Operate

Mars is their home. The Hungarian-named Martian cities are their territory. By Book 7 they lead the return to Earth.

## Character

Fierce independence born from centuries of cultural stubbornness under foreign domination. They fight not for conquest but to be left alone — which maps to their key character László's personal desire.

## Key Character

**László** — the central protagonist. See `characters/laszlo.md`.

## Connections

- The Colombian e-thot eventually "turns coat" and decides to help the Resistance. She uses a Hungarian memelord to relay messages to László.
- The Resistance is the protagonist side in every Mafia vs. Earthtech conflict.

## Book 7 Role

After Earthtech is defeated, the Hungarians lead the resettlement of Earth. "Freedom to Live" — the final beat of the monomyth.
