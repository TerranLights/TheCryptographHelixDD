**OPENING IMAGE:**
The Venus gulags at the start of a prisoner work shift — the world of suffering that begins the entire series.

**SETUP:**
Mars colony life under Earthtech's control. The political prisoner pipeline from Earth to the Venus atmospheric mines. Earthtech develops suspicion that some inmates are finding ways to send messages off-world to Mars.

**CARE PACKAGE:**
A political prisoner in the Venus gulags — someone's father — encodes an encrypted message into flower DNA, adjusting the sequence to form a Fibonacci pattern. He sends many seeds to Mars, hoping one reaches the right person.
