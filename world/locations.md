# Locations

## Mars

At the series' opening, Mars is extensively developed and partially terraformed. It is the primary setting for Books 1–4.

### Martian Cities (Hungarian-Named)

The Martian colonies were founded by refugees from Tepenia whose descendants kept Hungarian cultural identity. City names:

- **New Szolnok** — major city; has been subject to organized crime; home to a GeoCistern (water/resource infrastructure)
- **Neo-Debrecen** — cultural hub; a talented programmer here gains solar-system fame; a hateful anti-terraforming protester ("Sons of Hephaestus") causes trouble
- **New Szeged** — near rich iridium deposits; prospectors flock here
- **New Turku** — a huge stone face was reported carved into nearby mountains during a dust storm, though it couldn't be found after
- **New Rovaniemi** — developing its own architectural style emphasizing stone surfaces and geometric proportions
- **Neo-Marosvásárhely** — hab dome degraded from Level V to Level IV due to fungus colonies
- **Neo-Zalaegerszeg** — buzz around a humanoid figure seen walking the surface without protection ("the Hiker")

### Martian Notes

- A hit TV show portrays New Szolnok as glamorous, causing a minor population boom
- Rich iridium deposits discovered near New Szeged — major economic boost
- Organized crime has spread through New Szolnok (early Mafia presence)

## Earth

San Francisco is the capital of Earth — headquarters of Earthtech. Countries and elections technically exist but are formalities. Earth Law-Enforcement is Earthtech's enforcement arm.

The story ends with the Hungarians leading a return to Earth after Earthtech's defeat.

## Venus

Venus has **floating gulags** above the atmosphere — political prisoners mined here as labor. The series opens with a shift-change at these gulags (Book 1).

The famous Fibonacci Flower is encoded by a prisoner's father in these gulags and sent to Mars.

*"Daylight was a lifetime on Venus if you walked fast enough. It was never fast enough. Hell, you could run, and you couldn't escape."* — opening sentence of the series

## The Belt (Asteroid Belt)

Mentioned as an active location in Books 3–6. Used for mining and infrastructure. Starlifters of the Sun are referenced nearby.

## Jupiter and the Jovian Metasystem

Jupiter colonization happens *during* the story (not before it). By the end of Book 1 / start of Book 2, Mafia cells are building infrastructure in the Jovian system.

- **Shellworlds** — artificial mini-planets built by the various Mafias in orbit around Jupiter. Each major Mafia has its own shellworld. Fully functional by Book 5.
- **Jovian moons** (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto) — mining bases. Ganymede Sunrise is a key visual image (Book 5). Storm over Callisto is the setting of Book 6.
- **Earth Law-Enforcement** notices spectral anomalies coming from Jupiter and investigates (end of Book 1's Mafia arc).

## Saturn

Mining bases on Titan and other Saturnian moons (Book 5 onward). The Mafias expand here before the ceasefire breaks.

## The Ship "Anicetus" *(possible name)*

An entire Arts & Culture district is under construction within this city-ship. Semi-constructed, semi-3D-printed. As it passes through the Belt and Jupiter/Saturn, the crew collects metal ores, stone, and natural resources (moonwater, etc.) for building.

## Starlifters

Solar starlifters are referenced from Books 2 onward. Revisited in Book 6 in the context of the DAMRAGE cult.
