# A breakdown of general topics per each book in the series

### Book #1:
- current tentative title: Terranocracy Anointed
- general idea: (Earthtech) having control over the people of Mars
- primary locations: Mars, Earth (also: Venus)

### Book #2:
- current tentative title: The Autonomy Allele
- general idea: Genetic Engineering and DNA Computation
- primary locations: Mars, Earth, Venus (also: starlifting the Sun, the Belt)

### Book #3:
- current tentative title: Neurotropic Dreamtime
- general idea: Mafia life, drug addiction, framejacking, and e-thottery
- primary locations: Mars, the Belt (also: Venus, starlifting the Sun; beginning the infrastructure around Jupiter)

### Book #4:
- current tentative title: The Architect Awakes
- general idea: The memes are alive and want self-determination
- primary locations: Mars, Earth, the Belt (also: Earth LaGrange with Moon and Sun; Jupiter towards the end)

### Book #5:
- current tentative title: Rays of a Ganymede Sunrise
- general idea: Isolationism and the ethics of lawlessness
- primary locations: Jupiter (also: Saturn)

### Book #6:
- current tentative title: Storm over Callisto
- general idea: Out-and-open Mafia warfare; the Resistance trying to check their power
- primary locations: Jupiter, Mars, the Belt

### Book #7:
- current tentative title: Embers of a Frostbitten Star
- general idea: Earthtech, the various mafias, and the Resistance against each other
- primary locations: Mars, Earth (also: Venus, the Belt, the starlifters)
