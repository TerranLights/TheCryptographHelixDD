## Continuity Anchor Checklist

These five questions must be answered with deliberate intent before Book 7 is drafted:

- [ ] Who writes the outer-worlds constitution at the end of Book 7?
- [ ] Which factions survive Book 7, and in what form?
- [ ] What do the Jovian shellworlds look like politically and culturally after the wars?
- [ ] What happens to Earthtech's infrastructure after their defeat?
- [ ] What is the status of the Memes by Book 7's end?

## Seed-Planting Checklist

Each item below is a moment in the novels that needs to be handled with care — they become mythology in Outer Tepenia:

- [ ] Fibonacci Flower — establish its founding-symbol potential in Book 7's resolution
- [ ] DAMRAGE cult — confirm their presence on the starlifters in Book 6 and whether they survive
- [ ] The mafioso who sacrificed his brain for his mother — confirm which book, which character, and give it narrative weight
- [ ] IoT assassination of "Elon Musk" — confirm which book and ensure it reads as a historical turning point
- [ ] Hungarian return to Earth (Book 7) — ensure the Altay Nation's political role is established clearly
- [ ] Russian Mafia's Techno-Primitivism — ensure it reads as a surviving philosophy, not just a tactic

## Inner Tepenia Consistency Check

Before finalizing any world-building element in the ~3000s Cryptograph Helix era, verify it doesn't contradict Inner Tepenia's established timeline (~2083–2840s). Key crossover points:
- The early solar system colonization referenced in Inner Tepenia must predate Cryptograph Helix's established colonies
- "Outer Tepenia" as a term originates in the Cryptograph Helix era — it should not appear in Inner Tepenia materials
- The Altay Nation (Hungarians + Mongolians + Kazakhs) in the Resistance has roots that Inner Tepenia may have laid groundwork for

## Phase 6 is Complete When

All 5 continuity anchors are resolved with deliberate answers. All 6 seed-planting items are confirmed in a specific book and given sufficient narrative weight to function as Outer Tepenia lore.
