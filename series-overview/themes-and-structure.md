# Themes and Structure

## Overarching Theme

**Freedom vs. Evolutionary Pressure (Man vs. Nature)**

The protagonist is not a person — it is **Freedom** itself. All seven books map the journey of Freedom through shadow and light, loss and reclamation.

The three philosophical sub-themes, cycling twice across the seven books:
1. **Privacy** (Books 1–2)
2. **Ethics** (Books 3–5)
3. **Power** (Books 6–7)

## 7-Book Arc: KWML and Monomyth

The series maps the Hero's Journey (Joseph Campbell's Monomyth) across all 7 books, with each book corresponding to a step of the Dan Harmon Story Circle. The KWML (King / Warrior / Magician / Lover) archetype system tracks the psychological state of "Freedom" across the arc.

| Book | Harmon Step | KWML State | Societal Cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You | Passive Shadow — King | Good Times |
| 2 | Need | Passive Shadow — King → Magician | Good Times → Weak Men |
| 3 | Go | Active Shadow — Magician | Weak Men |
| 4 | Search | Active Shadow — Magician → Lover | Weak Men → Hard Times |
| 5 | Find | Active Shadow — Lover → Warrior | Hard Times → Strong Men |
| 6 | Take/Pay | Light/Completion — Warrior | Strong Men |
| 7 | Return/Change | Light/Completion — Warrior | Strong Men → Good Times |

## Dan Harmon Story Circle (8 Steps)

Used for each faction's parallel arc and for each book's internal structure:

1. **You** — the status quo / zone of familiarity
2. **Need** — something isn't right / they want something
3. **Go** — cross the threshold / unfamiliar situation
4. **Search** — main tension / road of trials / adapt
5. **Find** — find what you need, but not as expected
6. **Pay** — pay a heavy price
7. **Return** — return, but there's a twist
8. **Change** — now a new person / master of both worlds

## 3-Act Structure (Per Book and Per Arc)

Each book and each faction's arc follows 3-act structure with these key beats:

**Act 1:** Disturbance → Care Package → Trouble Brewing → Argument Against Transformation → Doorway of No Return #1

**Act 2:** Kick in the Shins → Road of Trials → Pet the Dog → Mirror Moment (Midpoint) → Another Road of Trials → Doorway of No Return #2

**Act 3:** Mounting Forces → Lights Out → Q Factor → Final Battle → Transformation

> **Full beat-by-beat definitions** (with source attributions for Snyder/Bell/Truby/Campbell cross-references and exact percentage placements) are in `reference/story-structure-definitions.md`. That file also contains the blank template used for all per-book beat sheets. Terminology mapping: "Disturbance" = Catalyst; "Argument Against Transformation" = Debate; "Doorway of No Return #1/#2" = Break into Two/Three; "Mirror Moment" = Midpoint.

## Meme Symbolism and Jungian Depth Psychology

Memes communicate via Jungian subconscious symbols. The conflict between a meme's symbolic language and a human host's own natural subconscious symbolism can cause **psychoschizoid breaks**. By Book 7, humans integrating the memes represents integration of the collective Shadow — a Jungian individuation at the species level.

The Final Battle in Book 7: humans integrate their memes (collective Shadow); convince the memes to integrate their own Shadow; then unite to fight Earthtech — who are too egotistical (Satanic, in the Jungian sense) to even acknowledge they have a Shadow.
