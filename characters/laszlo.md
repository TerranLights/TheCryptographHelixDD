# László

## Basic Info

- **Name:** László
- **Nationality / Ethnicity:** Hungarian
- **Faction:** The Resistance / Altay Nation
- **Role in story:** Central protagonist
- **First appears:** Book 2 (~event 2.10); his personal story circle begins at event 4.1

## Background

*"Swear to myself I'd only die one time; brought back from death; even failed at that."*

László has been brought back from death. This single detail defines him — not as a hero reborn with purpose, but as a man who couldn't even get dying right. The nihilism is dark but not hollow; it sits alongside an unresolved question about absurdism.

## Personality and Character Type

Deep independence. Wants to be left alone. Not a natural leader — but people follow him anyway, and he doesn't understand why (he does this unknowingly).

His nihilism vs. absurdism internal conflict: Is life meaningless (nihilism), or is it meaningless and worth living anyway (absurdism)? His arc is the resolution of this question through action.
