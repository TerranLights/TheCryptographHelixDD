# Chinese Mafia — Overview

## Identity

The Chinese Mafia are masters of deception, concealment, and long-term hegemony. Unlike the Russian Mafia's Techno-Primitivism, the Chinese invest heavily in cutting-edge technology — particularly brain-interface encryption. Their drugs are not designed to give people a good time; they are engineered to addict and eventually destroy the user, establishing Chinese dominance.

## What They Traffic

- **Weapon schematics** — DNA-encoded blueprints for weapons
- **Designer babies** — engineered genetics on demand
- **Drug schematics** — addictive, destructive drugs designed to step on the Russian market

## Technology Specialty

**Brainwave encryption using the SCN/TMN biological "private key":**
- The **suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)** — a subportion of the hypothalamus that serves as the brain's "master clock"
- The **tuberomamillary nucleus (TMN)** — a localized "sleep clock"
- Together (STN system), they generate a biological rhythm unique to each person, used as a cryptographic private key

Chinese mafiosos hide their computer activities by encrypting them behind this biological rhythm. The Chinese were the first to develop this technique.

## Key Events

- **IoT assassination of an innocent Russian man** — The Chinese committed this killing. The victim was:
  - A childhood friend of a Russian Mafia boss
  - A member of the DAMRAGE cult (non-violent, Buddhist-adjacent)
  - The lover of the Colombian e-thot's sister
  - This assassination triggers catastrophic inter-Mafia conflict

- **Chinese drugs stepping on Russian markets** — The Russians get exceedingly angry when the Chinese begin trafficking drug schematics into their territory

- **Russian counter-hack** — The Russians use meme-warfare to hack the SCN/TMN private key and render Chinese mafiosos brainfucked

## The Colombian Connection

The Chinese mafioso who ordered the IoT assassination of the innocent Russian man is also one of the Colombian e-thot's best clients. When she learns what he did, she is appalled and begins her turn toward the Resistance.
