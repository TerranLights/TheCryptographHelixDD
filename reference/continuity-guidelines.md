# Continuity Guidelines

Rules and watchpoints for keeping The Cryptograph Helix internally consistent and properly set up for the Outer Tepenia RPG series that follows it.

---

## Continuity Anchors

Decisions made in Cryptograph Helix that the Outer Tepenia games will need to honor. These must be settled deliberately — not left vague — before the novel series is considered complete.

- **Who writes the outer-worlds constitution at the end of Book 7?** The political structure that results from this document is the founding law of Outer Tepenia's civilization.
- **Which factions survive Book 7, and in what form?** The Mafias, the Resistance, the Memes — their post-war status determines who the power players are when Outer Tepenia 1 opens.
- **What do the Jovian shellworlds look like politically and culturally after the wars?** Their governance model, their scars, their relationship to Earth and Mars — all foundational to the RPG setting.
- **What happens to Earthtech's infrastructure after their defeat?** The surveillance systems, the Venus gulags, the android leadership — does any of it survive, get repurposed, go underground?
- **What is the status of the Memes by Book 7's end?** Symbiotic with humanity — but what does that look like practically? Do they have legal personhood? Territories? This matters enormously for Outer Tepenia.

---

## Seed-Planting

Moments in the novels that can pay off as ancient legends, historical touchstones, or lore in the Outer Tepenia games. Handle these with care — they are the mythology of the later era.

- **The Fibonacci Flower** — a father's encrypted message from a Venus gulag. Could be a founding symbol of the freedom movement; possibly a relic or artifact in Outer Tepenia.
- **The DAMRAGE cult** — present on the starlifters in Book 6. Could survive into Outer Tepenia as a philosophical tradition or religious order.
- **The mafioso who sacrificed his brain to save his mother** — a story of personal loyalty over institutional obligation. Could become a legend or folk hero in outer-worlds culture.
- **The IoT assassination of "Elon Musk"** — the first time memes acted collectively as a political force. A historical turning point that Outer Tepenia historians would study.
- **The Hungarian return to Earth (Book 7)** — the Altay Nation leading terrestrial resettlement. Their role in Outer Tepenia's political landscape flows directly from this moment.
- **The Russian Mafia's Techno-Primitivism** — a philosophy that survives wars. Could be a cultural movement or faction identity in Outer Tepenia.

---

## Scope Creep Watch

When an idea is exciting but belongs in Outer Tepenia rather than Cryptograph Helix, flag it and park it rather than forcing it into the novels. The novels have to end cleanly enough for the games to have a foundation to build on.

- Cryptograph Helix ends with the *establishment* of outer-worlds independence — not its full flowering. That flowering is Outer Tepenia's story.
- Jupiter colonization happens *during* Cryptograph Helix. Saturn colonization is underway by Book 5. Anything beyond that (further outer planets, interstellar precursors) belongs to later entries.
- Interstellar travel and New Centauri are the endpoint of the full universe arc — do not introduce them, even as hints, before Outer Tepenia 2 at the earliest.

---

## Timeline Integrity

- The gap between Cryptograph Helix (~3000s–3100s) and Outer Tepenia 1 is TBD but likely several generations — enough for the post-war civilization to have settled and for the events of the novels to have become history rather than living memory.
- Everything established in Cryptograph Helix must be consistent with Inner Tepenia's timeline (~2083–2840s). See `series-overview/universe-timeline.md`.
- Do not lock a specific opening date for Cryptograph Helix until world-building context demands it.
