## Overall Topic

Earthtech having control over the people of Mars.

## Beat Sheet

### Act 1

**Setting (Total Story context):**
- The floating gulags over the atmosphere of Venus

**Disturbance:**
- Wake-up call happens for a particular shift of political prisoners from Earth beginning to work in the Venus gulags

**Care Package:**
- Someone's father (a Venus prisoner) encodes an encrypted message into flower DNA and sends it to Mars hoping someone will figure it out. He adjusts the DNA to make it a **Fibonacci Flower**. He sends many seeds, hoping one reaches the right person.

**Trouble Brewing:**
- Earthtech has developed suspicion that some inmates are figuring out ways to send messages off-world to Mars, to tell people what's really happening in the gulags

**Call to Adventure:**
- A kid on Mars decodes the encrypted message in the Fibonacci Flower DNA. The message reads: **"They're killing us."**

**Argument Against Transformation / Refusal of Call:**
- Those mines are heavily fortified. There's no way anybody can help them.

**Supernatural Aid / Doorway of No Return #1:**
- One of the Venus inmates sends an encrypted password. He's a former Earthtech employee who was betrayed by Howie.
