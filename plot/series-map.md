# Series Map — Freedom Arc (3-Act Structure)

The overall 3-act structure of the entire series, with Freedom as the protagonist.

---

## Act 1

**Disturbance:**
- Earthtech's spying on people becomes known

**Care Package:**
- The Fibonacci Flower: a father (Venus prisoner) encodes an encrypted message into flower DNA and sends many seeds to Mars. One reaches someone who decodes it: *"They're killing us."* This flower returns as the Q Factor in Act 3 (Book 7).

**Trouble Brewing:**
- Earthtech develops suspicion that inmates are sending messages off-world

**Argument Against Transformation:**
- Those mines are heavily fortified. There's no way anybody can help them.

**Doorway of No Return #1:**
- The very first DNA Mafia forms (Russian or Chinese — TBD which is truly first)

**Lights Out:**
- Freedom being total self-actualization at every scale; "Lights Out" = near-total willing, voluntary submission

**Q Factor:**
- The Venus mines (a physical object — the place it all started)
- Responsibility (a rebuke from moral authority — "what has moral authority over Freedom? Responsibility")

**Final Battle:**
- Humans integrate the memes (their collective Shadow)
